6) [Running a One Trillion-Parameter LLM Locally on AMD Ryzen AI Max+ Cluster（AMD）](https://www.amd.com/en/developer/resources/technical-articles/2026/how-to-run-a-one-trillion-parameter-llm-locally-an-amd.html)

- 亮点：对企业私有化部署而言，可先验证“多机推理TCO vs 云端调用”再决定是否自建推理集群。
